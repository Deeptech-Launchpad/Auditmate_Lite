{# Why a person does this and the app does not. Being a director's wife is not a property of a ledger entry. The books record relatedness nowhere, and on this client every entry in the director loan account is Spend Money or Receive Money with no counterparty at all. So the list is given, the app puts what looks like it belongs to someone on that list in front of a person, and a person decides. A name that merely looks similar is how an unrelated supplier ends up disclosed as a director's company. #}
